**Status: idea under study.** No live network, no token for sale, no returns promised.

## The idea
Start from Monero's open source code and its logic, that is ring signatures, stealth addresses and confidential transactions, to build a decentralized digital currency of our own. To understand how Monero works: [Monero (XMR): what it is and how it works](https://cialdecompatibili-netizen.github.io/cryptomagazine/blog/enciclopedia/monero-xmr-cose-e-come-funziona/).

## Why a fork
- We start from technology the community has developed and watched for many years, instead of writing everything from scratch.
- We can change what we need (parameters, name, network rules) while keeping the base logic.

## What must be clear before building
1. **License**: check the terms of Monero's code license and comply with them in the fork.
2. **Purpose and users**: who needs this currency and why they would use it instead of Monero.
3. **Network**: who runs the nodes and who mines, because a coin with no participants does not exist.
4. **Rules**: privacy-oriented coins have limits, such as exclusion from some exchanges and EU crypto-asset rules. Legal review is needed before any fundraising.
5. **Security**: audit the modified code before launching a real network.
