Here we collect the decentralized projects we follow. Each card shows the real status: idea, under study, in development.

## Private coin
**Idea under study.** A decentralized digital currency with privacy built in: ring signatures, one-time addresses and hidden amounts. [Read the full guide]({{ '/en/projects/private-coin/' | relative_url }}).

## Immutable ledger
**Idea under study.** Certification, traceability and notarization services: the fingerprint of a file is recorded (never the file or personal data) and you get proof of date and integrity. First planned tool: "stamp a file". [Project page](https://cialdecompatibili-netizen.github.io/startupfinance/en/startup/immutable-registry/).

## Physical network
**Idea under study.** Physical things linked to a ledger with automatic payment: parking spots bookable by phone, drones for inspections and surveys, objects with QR/NFC (maintenance, parcels, rentals). First planned test: one private car park and a single drone service. [Project page](https://cialdecompatibili-netizen.github.io/startupfinance/en/startup/physical-network/).
